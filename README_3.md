# Insurer Views on Distribution Reforms

An interactive, single-page comparison of:

- **18 life insurers' submissions** to the Life Insurance Council on IRDAI's Consultation Paper *Recalibrating Economics of Insurance Distribution* (Sep 2026), and
- **analysis from 13 brokerage houses** on the same paper,

sorted into 14 areas (EoM, credit life, pension & annuity, ULIP, bancassurance and more). Insurer asks are shown in blue and broker analysis in green.

## Views
- **By area**: every insurer's ask and every broker's view on one topic, side by side.
- **By company**: one insurer or broker across all areas.
- **Overview**: a grid of who commented on what.
- Filter by All / Insurers / Brokers, and search across all text.

## Hosting on GitHub Pages
1. Create a new repository and upload `index.html`, `.nojekyll`, `robots.txt` and this `README.md` to the root.
2. Go to **Settings → Pages**, set **Source** to *Deploy from a branch*, pick `main` and `/ (root)`, and save.
3. The site will be live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/` within a minute or two.

The page is fully self-contained (all data is inside `index.html`); only Google Fonts load from the web.

## Notes
- Sources: the Council's note *Views of Life Insurers on Distribution* (with each insurer's attachment) and brokerage research notes dated Aug–Oct 2026.
- Goldman Sachs and J.P. Morgan notes are excluded, as their terms prohibit use of their research in third-party AI tools.
- `robots.txt` and a `noindex` tag ask search engines not to index the page. This does **not** make it private: anyone with the URL can open it.
